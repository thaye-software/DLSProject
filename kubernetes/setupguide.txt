



---------- RabbitMQ, first workload (2026-11-06) ----------

Software setup is in softwareToInstall.txt. This is the first thing that runs on the cluster.
RabbitMQ is the broker in microservices/docker-compose.yml. Catalog, Orders, Search, Payment, and Notif
connect with amqp://guest:guest@rabbitmq:5672. The image rabbitmq:4-management is public, so nothing is built or pushed.

Files:
kubernetes/rabbitmq/configmap.yaml
kubernetes/rabbitmq/deployment.yaml
kubernetes/rabbitmq/service.yaml

configmap.yaml sets loopback_users.guest = false. Without that, guest is accepted only from inside
the RabbitMQ container, and the other services cannot log in through the Service.

The Service is a NodePort:
AMQP from the laptop:        localhost:30672
Management UI from the laptop: http://localhost:31672
AMQP from another pod:       rabbitmq:5672
Login: guest / guest

From the repo root, in a normal PowerShell:

kubectl apply -f kubernetes/rabbitmq
kubectl rollout status deployment/rabbitmq
kubectl get pods -l app=rabbitmq
kubectl get svc rabbitmq

A ready pod and a Service with ports 30672 and 31672 means it is up.
Open http://localhost:31672 and sign in as guest / guest.
